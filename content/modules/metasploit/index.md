Metasploit is the Swiss-army knife of exploitation — a modular framework that handles the entire kill chain from recon to post-ex. Mastering it means knowing when to lean on it for bulk work and when to drop out to manual tooling. This module covers the architecture, workflow, payload generation with msfvenom, meterpreter post-ex, evasion tactics, and dropping your own modules into the framework.
